About the app

Tech UP (com.techup) is an independent news aggregator. Its legacy app lets readers choose a technology news source, follow article links, and save bookmarks on their device. Tech UP is not the publisher of the third-party articles it links to and is not affiliated with those publishers.

Original articles and their authorship belong to their respective publishers. Editorial corrections to an original article should be directed to that publisher; app display and attribution problems can be reported to the developer above.

News sources

The legacy app offered sources including TechCrunch, Ars Technica, The Verge, Engadget and Hacker News. These links open the original services, where you can find their current content and publisher information.

Source availability in the legacy app may differ from this directory while maintenance is underway.
